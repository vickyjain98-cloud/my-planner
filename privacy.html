Privacy policy

My Planner · Effective 17 November 2026 · Contact: vickyjain98@gmail.com

In short: My Planner has no server and no database of its own. What you write is kept on your device and in a “My Planner” folder in your own Google Drive. We cannot see it. There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics.

Who we are

My Planner is a personal planner app made by Vicky Jain (Gurgaon, India). In this policy “we” means the maker of the app, and “you” means the person using it.

What the app uses and why

  • Your Google account name, email address and profile ID — to greet you, to keep each person's planner separate on a shared device, and to check that your account is allowed to use the app. These stay on your device.
  • Google Drive (only files the app creates) — the app saves your planner in files it creates in a “My Planner” folder in your Drive, so it syncs across your devices. It uses Google's drive.file permission, which means it cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar (only if you use it) — when you choose to send an item (a reminder, task, study session, event, birthday or anniversary) the app creates that event in your calendar, and can delete events it created. It does not read your other calendar events. This permission is asked for only the first time you send something.
  • Google Docs (only if you turn it on) — if you switch on the family summary or the shared suggestions document, the app creates that document in your Drive and shares it, view-only, with the email addresses you enter.
  • What you enter — timetable, habits, study log, to-dos, events, journal, affirmations, handwriting, mood and settings. This is your content. It is stored only on your device and in your own Drive.

What we do not do

  • We do not run a server, and we do not collect, receive, store or sell your data.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party cookies.
  • We do not use your data to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Nobody can see your planner unless you share something yourself.

Google API Services — Limited Use

My Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide those features or as you direct (for example sharing a summary document), and is never used for advertising.

Sign-in and security

  • Sign-in uses Google's own sign-in. The access token is kept only in memory while the app is open and is never written to disk.
  • All traffic between the app and Google is encrypted (HTTPS). The app only connects to Google's sign-in and API addresses.
  • Everything you type is treated as plain text, so pasted content cannot run as code.

Suggestions you send

If you use the Suggestions screen, your suggestion is saved in your own planner, and the app prepares an email to the maker that you choose to send from your own email app. That email (and your email address) is then received by the maker and used only to improve the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays until you delete it. Settings → Delete all my data removes the app's Drive files and everything on the device.
  • You can also download a backup or a CSV at any time from Settings.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Events already added to your Google Calendar stay there until you remove them (the app offers a Remove button for each).

Children

My Planner is not directed at children under 13. Teenagers should use it with a parent's or guardian's knowledge, in line with Google's account rules in their country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the app's “What's new” note will mention it.
